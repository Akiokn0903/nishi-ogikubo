You don't have to go far to discover somewhere new.
西荻窪には、まだ知らない場所がある。

A town is more than a list of places.

It is the people who live there. The food they love. The old stories, small rituals, hidden rooms and streets that somehow feel different at dusk. This is an attempt to collect those things — together.
